## 1. Kharif Season Sowing Status:

-  Kharif  sowing  data  collection  has  started  from  05 th June,  2026  and  the  sowing  is  in  preliminary stage. ## 2. Weather Update:

- a) Temperature: Maximum temperature more than 5.1°C (Markedly above normal) at few places over  Sub  Himalayan  West  Bengal  &amp;  Sikkim,  at  isolated  places  over  Chhattisgarh,  Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal.
- b) Rainfall  Distribution:  From  01  June  to  12 July  2026,  the  All-India  cumulative  rainfall  is  18% lower than its Long Period Average (LPA), with regional variations: (i) East &amp; Northeast India: (-) 37%; (ii) Northwest India: -9%; (iii) Central India: -5%; and South Peninsula: -20%.
3.  Heavy rainfall at isolated places over Assam, Meghalaya and East Uttar Pradesh.
4.  Fairly  widespread  to  widespread  rainfall  over  Andaman  &amp;  Nicobar,  West  Bengal,  Sikkim, North-East India, Keral, Lakshadweep and coastal Karnataka from 13-18 th July 2026.
5.  Thunderstorm  with  gusty  wind  over  J&amp;K,  Ladakh,  Andaman  Nicobar,  Jharkhand,  Bihar, Coastal Andhra Pradesh and Telangana from 13-18 th July 2026.
6.  Hot and Humid conditions likely to prevail over Odisha on 13 th July, Coastal Tamil Nadu during 13-14 th July 2026.
7.  Heat wave conditions very likely in isolated pockets over Coastal Andhra Pradesh &amp; Yaman during 13-14 th July 2026.

## 3. Inputs Situation

- a) Water  Reservoir  Status :  As  on  9  July  2026,  the  total  live  water  storage  in  166  important reservoirs across the country (monitored by CWC) is 59.443 billion cubic meters, which is 32.38% of total live storage capacity (183.565 BCM). The current year's storage is nearly 63.52 percent of last  year's  storage  and  107.62  percent  of  the  average  storage  of  the  last  ten  years  during  the corresponding period. The overall storage position is less than the corresponding period of last year in the country as a whole but is better than the Normal storage during the corresponding period.
- b) Groundwater Levels: The groundwater situation is largely stable across the country. Out of 9,085 monitoring wells,  87.8%  (7,977  wells)  fall  under  the  normal  category,  while  12.2%  of  the  wells exhibit varying levels of groundwater drought.

Approximately  69%  of  the  wells  have  groundwater  levels  within  10  metres  below  ground  level, whereas  only  about  4%  have  groundwater  levels  deeper  than  40  metres, indicating  good groundwater availability across most regions.

Groundwater  drought  is  predominantly  mild  and  confined  to  limited  areas.  Among  the  affected wells,  7.2%  are  classified  as  experiencing  mild  drought,  2.2%  moderate  drought,  0.9%  severe drought, and 1.9% extreme drought.

## Key Insights

According to the Pre-Monsoon 2026 Groundwater Drought Index, groundwater conditions across the country are predominantly normal, with groundwater stress restricted to limited areas in a few states.
